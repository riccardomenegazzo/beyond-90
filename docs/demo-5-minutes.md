# Showing Beyond 90 in five minutes

A script for explaining the project out loud. It uses the built-in demo customer, which is invented, so you can show it anywhere.

Keep to one story: a customer who says "we are at 90%" and is not.

**Minute 1: the question.** Open the live page and press the demo button. Say: "A customer tells you 90% of their accounts are connected. I wanted to know what that number leaves out." Point at Today, where one sentence says where the customer really stands.

**Minute 2: why the number moves.** Open Accounts. The page opens with the answer in a sentence and the few accounts to look at first, each with the reason. Say what you would do with the first one, as you would on a call.

**Minute 3: what to do next.** Open Actions. Three cards, each with the effect in points and the hours it takes. Open one and read the "done when" line. Say that the effects are estimates meant for ranking, not for adding up. People trust you more when you say so first.

**Minute 4: the conversation.** Open Prep a call. Show what to say and what to ask. Then Risk burn-down: how much risk was closed, and how much of it by hand, with a tool's help or automatically.

**Minute 5: what it will not do.** Say plainly: the data is invented, nothing leaves the browser, and the security review was done by the author, not by an outside firm. Then say what you would do next with a real customer.

## If they ask

- *Is it only for one vendor?* No. The profiles cover cloud security, endpoint, SaaS and AI security, and you can write your own.
- *Where does the data go?* Nowhere. It stays in the browser and can be erased per customer.
- *Why did you build it?* Because adoption reports flatter. I wanted one that could not.
