# Promises and the handoff note

## Promises

**Promises** (the tab of that name) keeps what you and your customers have promised each other, across all your customers: what is late, what is due this week, what is open, what you owe, and what you closed in the last 30 days. Mark a promise done or drop it; reopen it if you were wrong. **Export CSV** gives the whole ledger.

After a call, **Keep in the ledger** (on the *On the call* screen) adds what was agreed, with who and by when, and what you owe. A promise already open with the same words for the same customer is not added again.

Promises are kept in this browser, in their own store. If you set a passphrase, they are sealed with it like saved snapshots. They travel in an exported workspace file (under `commitments`) and come back on import; each one is checked on the way in and a file with more than 5,000 is cut off.

The ledger is yours, not a shared system. It does not remind you, and nothing is sent to the customer.

## Handoff note

**Handoff note** builds one page for whoever takes the customer over: where adoption stands and the three best next actions, what has been promised and what is late, renewal signals and the people (with how recently each was in touch), how Yield has moved, risks to watch, and earlier renewals. Fill in *From* and *To* if you want them named. **Copy** or **Download .md**.

It is built from what the page holds when you open it: the reading, the ledger, the saved readings and the renewal log. If a part has nothing behind it, the note says so instead of leaving it out. It never includes your private call notes. The health indicator is called a hypothesis, as it is everywhere. Read the note before you hand it over: it is a draft for a person, not a record that anyone has verified.
