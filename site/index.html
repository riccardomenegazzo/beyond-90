For technical account managers

Adoption you can't flatter.

Beyond 90 measures how much of a security product a customer really uses, finds the gaps behind the number, and plans the hours and the conversations to close them, for every customer in your book, every week.

  • Runs in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.
  • Open source, Apache-2.0, no vendor.
  • Works offline, installs nothing.

A score that can't be gamed

Yield multiplies four honest stages against their own denominators, and shows the gap to the flattering number.

The week, in order

Top and mid-tier customers ranked with the reason for each place, a prepared agenda for every call.

News that touches them

Advisories matched to each customer's inventory, with a note you can send in plain words.

Private by design

Strict policy, Trusted Types, optional passphrase for saved data. Your files never leave the tab.

Drop a customer folder here or choose files (customer.toml, accounts.csv, …)
